# Lost Spot

A short interactive-fiction game made in Twine (Harlowe). You come home on a hot afternoon to find your family's dog, Spot, gone, and set out across town to find him before Mom finds out. Not everything in town is quite what it seems.

**[▶ Play it in your browser](https://colinhuang314.github.io/Lost-Spot-Twine-/)**

## How it works
- **75 passages and 10 endings.** Where you go, and in what order, decides which ending you reach.
- **Hidden state behind the story.** Variables track your energy and a hidden "suspicion" score that rises as you explore strange places. Higher suspicion changes what you notice in later scenes and opens up other endings.
- **Memory of where you've been.** Passages check which places you have already visited, so descriptions and choices change on a second visit.
- **Your name.** The game asks for your name at the start and uses it in the story.

Inspired by *80 Days*, where hidden numbers quietly shape the story you read.
